# Residual Risk

**Definition:** Residual risk is the risk that remains after current or planned controls are applied.

## Key points

- Possible treatments: avoid, reduce/mitigate, transfer/share financial effect or accept under delegated authority.
- Risk transfer does not erase business, legal or reputational responsibility.
- Document the remaining failure scenarios, owner, decision authority, conditions and scheduled review.

## AI security example

> [!example] Application
> A prompt-injection filter lowers risk but cannot guarantee zero failures; tool permissions and monitoring may reduce the remaining consequence.
