# Cloud Shared Responsibility

**Definition:** Shared responsibility allocates cybersecurity duties between a cloud provider and its customer, depending on the service and deployment model.

## Key points

- IaaS customers manage more of operating system/runtime; PaaS shares application/platform duties; SaaS customers still own identity, data governance and tenant configuration.
- Provider-managed infrastructure does not guarantee secure APIs, correct permissions, log retention or lawful processing.
- Check contracts, regional processing, subprocessors, incident responsibilities and exit/migration processes.

## AI security example

> [!example] Application
> For managed AI APIs, the provider secures its platform; the department must still authorize prompts/data and protect its integration credentials.
