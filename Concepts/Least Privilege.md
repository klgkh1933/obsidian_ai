# Least Privilege

**Definition:** Least privilege means granting only the rights and time needed for a justified task.

## Key points

- Use task-scoped permissions, time limits, separation of human and service identities and regular reviews.
- Remove standing administrator access and stale privileges; account for privilege inheritance.
- The security objective is to minimize the consequences of account or tool compromise.

## AI security example

> [!example] Application
> An AI assistant may search authorized documents but cannot send emails or run shell commands unless those capabilities are explicitly required.
