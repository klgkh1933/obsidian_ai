# Separation of Duties

**Definition:** Separation of duties divides sensitive steps among independent people or roles to reduce error, misuse and fraud.

## Key points

- Separate requesting, approving, implementing and independently reviewing high-risk changes.
- Apply at human and automation levels; avoid one agent being able to request, approve and execute its own privileged action.
- Document emergency exceptions and post-event review.

## AI security example

> [!example] Application
> One AI service proposes a privileged account change, but a human with authority must approve it and the system logs execution.
