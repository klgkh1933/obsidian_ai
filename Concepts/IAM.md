# IAM

**Definition:** Identity and Access Management (IAM) controls the registration, authentication, authorization, review and retirement of identities.

## Key points

- Manage human, privileged, emergency, service and workload identities separately.
- Revoke access when roles change, limit standing privilege and review role/group inheritance.
- MFA, phishing-resistant passkeys, federated identity and secure recovery increase assurance, but require sound authorization.

## AI security example

> [!example] Application
> An AI workload has a managed identity; per-user permissions still constrain what it may retrieve from a document store.
