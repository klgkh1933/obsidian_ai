# Data Lifecycle

**Definition:** The data lifecycle spans collection, inventory, classification, use, sharing, retention and secure disposal, including secondary copies.

## Key points

- Track the data owner, lawful/authorized uses, location, copies and processors at each stage.
- Apply approved retention rules and preserve records subject to legal holds.
- Verify disposal of backups, caches, indices and obsolete copies as appropriate; do not assume deleting the source deletes downstream embeddings.
