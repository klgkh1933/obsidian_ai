# Risk Assessment

**Definition:** Security risk assessment analyzes what could go wrong, how likely it is, its consequences and how effectively controls reduce it.

## Key points

- Describe asset, threat, vulnerability, attack scenario, likelihood, impact and current safeguards.
- SLE = asset value × exposure factor; ARO = events/year; ALE = SLE × ARO where reliable estimates exist.
- Recognize uncertainty; severity scores such as CVSS do not determine organization-specific risk automatically.
- Assign risk ownership, risk treatment, review triggers and evidence.

## AI security example

> [!example] Application
> An AI assistant may disclose protected documents: evaluate likelihood through access tests and impact through sensitivity and reach.
