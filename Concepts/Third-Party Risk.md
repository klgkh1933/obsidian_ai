# Third-Party Risk

**Definition:** Third-party risk arises when an organization depends on providers, suppliers or subprocessors whose failure or compromise can affect its security.

## Key points

- Perform due diligence before acquisition, include security requirements in contracts, monitor suppliers and plan secure exit.
- Check rights to audit, incident reporting, SLA, data use/retention and processor/subprocessor obligations.
- Supply-chain assurance includes model weights, training/embedding data sources and dependencies.

## AI security example

> [!example] Application
> A hosted LLM provider may retain prompts or use subcontractors; verify contracts and approved data-handling restrictions.
