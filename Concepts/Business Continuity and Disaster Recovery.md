# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery

**Definition:** Business continuity maintains critical business functions through disruption; disaster recovery restores affected technology and data.

## Key points

- Plan redundancy, secondary locations, backup strategy, restoration and crisis communications.
- Hot/warm/cold sites trade readiness, cost and setup time.
- Test practical recovery steps and prove dependency availability, not merely existence of backups.

## AI security example

> [!example] Application
> An AI provider outage may require degraded non-AI processing to preserve critical government service delivery.
