CIA Triad:
- Confidentiality 
	- Only the right people
	- Encryption
	- Access control
- Integrity 
	- No unauthorized change
	- Hashing
		- Temper-evident seal 
- Availability 
	- Up when you need it
	- Backups, redundancy
	- Failover, patching
DAD Triad:
- Disclosure <> failure of CIA.Confidentiality
- Alteration <> failure of CIA.Integrity
- Destruction <> failure of CIA.Availability
