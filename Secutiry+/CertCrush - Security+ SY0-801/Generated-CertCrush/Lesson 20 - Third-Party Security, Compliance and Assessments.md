# Lesson 20 — Third-Party Security, Compliance and Assessments

**[▶ Watch video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtfopUHES4Q)** · [[009 - Security+ SY0-801 Third-Party Risk and Compliance (Lesson 20)|Full transcript]] · [[Security+ - Master Notes|Consolidated notes]] · [[Index|All lessons]]

> [!summary] Learning target — Essential
> Understand the concepts and be able to explain **why the safeguards work, where they fail, and how to verify them**. These are synthesized study notes, **not** the video transcript.

## Vendor lifecycle · [↗ 00:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtfopUHES4Q&t=55s)

- [[Third-Party Risk]]: establish requirements → due diligence → contract → continuously monitor → offboard safely.
- Assess model/data provenance, subcontractors, hosting location, security posture, retention and incident handling.
- SLAs specify measurable service levels; rights to audit, reporting duties and exit arrangements help enforce expectations.

## Privacy, compliance and data handling · [↗ 05:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtfopUHES4Q&t=303s)

- Compliance means meeting applicable statutes, directives, standards, contract obligations and evidence requirements.
- Privacy roles (controller/processor/subprocessor) are legal-context dependent; do not transfer foreign regulatory wording to Canadian federal operations automatically.
- Protect access requests, retention/holds, erasure and cross-border transfers according to applicable law and departmental policy.

## Audit, evaluation and testing · [↗ 09:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtfopUHES4Q&t=584s)

- Internal/external audits inspect control design and operating effectiveness; assertions need evidence.
- [[Security Assessment and Authorization]]: define scope and requirements, review implementation, test controls, report findings and obtain authorized risk decisions.
- Penetration testing is authorized and scoped; functional testing and attack simulation answer different questions.

## Awareness and people · [↗ 14:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtfopUHES4Q&t=849s)

- Train staff on real workflows; measure improvement using reporting rates, simulated exercises and follow-up actions.
- Engage privacy, legal, procurement, technical security, data owners and executives in decisions.


## Apply it to AI security assurance

> [!example] Scenario
> Assess external AI vendors’ data-use restrictions, logging, support access, model changes, jurisdiction and exit procedures.
