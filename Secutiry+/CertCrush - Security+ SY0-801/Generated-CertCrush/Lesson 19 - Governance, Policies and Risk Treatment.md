# Lesson 19 — Governance, Policies and Risk Treatment

**[▶ Watch video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QNT9oLfo-3I)** · [[008 - Security+ SY0-801 Policy vs Standard vs Procedure (Lesson 19)|Full transcript]] · [[Security+ - Master Notes|Consolidated notes]] · [[Index|All lessons]]

> Understand the concepts and be able to explain **why the safeguards work, where they fail, and how to verify them**. These are synthesized study notes, **not** the video transcript.

## Documents: intent through execution · [↗ 01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QNT9oLfo-3I&t=97s)

- [[Security Governance]]: **policy** states mandatory high-level direction; **standard** gives specific mandatory requirements.
- **Procedure/SOP/runbook** gives repeatable steps; **guideline** offers recommended practices; **baseline** establishes approved minimum configuration.
- Ensure roles, authority, version control, exceptions and evidence are defined.

## Frameworks · [↗ 06:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QNT9oLfo-3I&t=406s)

- NIST CSF 2.0 functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover.
- ISO 27001 supports an information-security management system; SOC 2 reports assess service-provider controls.
- Framework vocabulary is transferable, but the Canadian GC policy and departmental requirements govern your actual job.

## Risk assessment and calculations · [↗ 09:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QNT9oLfo-3I&t=574s)

- [[Risk Assessment]]: identify threat, asset, vulnerability, likelihood, consequence, existing controls and residual risk.
- SLE = asset value × exposure factor; ARO = estimated frequency/year; ALE = SLE × ARO.
- Quantification helps compare choices, but inputs are uncertain; document assumptions and use qualitative methods when appropriate.

## Treatment and authority · [↗ 12:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QNT9oLfo-3I&t=763s)

- Risk treatments: **avoid**, **mitigate/reduce**, **transfer/share** financial effects, or **accept** explicitly.
- Risk appetite guides limits; risk owner and approver must have clear delegated authority.
- [[Residual Risk]] remains after controls; report it rather than claiming a system is fully secure.
