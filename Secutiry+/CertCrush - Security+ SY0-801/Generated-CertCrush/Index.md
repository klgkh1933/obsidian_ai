## Recommended active-learning process

1. **Preview** the lesson note for 1–2 minutes; choose a concrete question you want answered.
2. **Watch** video segments; pause only when an idea is unclear or when you need to verify an example.
3. **Recall** without looking: define 3–5 ideas and explain a design trade-off out loud.
4. **Apply**: answer the AI-supervisor scenario and practical questions from the note.
5. **Record only your additions** in your personal note: misconceptions, examples, disputed claims and follow-up questions.
6. **Review** after 1 day, ~1 week and later according to errors; focus on topics you could not recall.
